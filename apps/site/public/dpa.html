Data processing agreement

OpenParley Cloud · Last updated {{legal.updated}}

This data processing agreement (DPA, in Dutch a verwerkersovereenkomst) is part of the terms of service between you, the customer who runs an OpenParley Cloud workspace, and {{legal.provider}} (OpenParley, we). You are the controller of the personal data in your workspace; we are your processor. Words like personal data, processing and data breach mean what they mean in the GDPR (EU Regulation 2016/679). If this DPA and the terms disagree about personal data, this DPA wins.

1. What we process

Purpose
Providing the Service: storing and showing threads, comments and accounts, sending notification emails, and giving coding agents access.
People
Your members; people mentioned or named in comments; and anyone whose data appears in the element details the overlay records from your pages.
Data
Name, email address, mention name, role, password hash; comments and anything written in them; page paths, element selectors, up to 120 characters of element text and some element attributes; environment and revision; timestamps; notification settings; names of connected agents; hashed sessions and tokens; IP addresses, in memory only, to limit sign-in attempts.
Duration
As long as the agreement runs, and afterwards until the data is deleted (section 9).

You make sure you are allowed to have this data processed, and that no special categories of personal data are put into the Service.

2. Your instructions

We process the data only on your instructions: the terms, this DPA, and what you and your members do in the Service. We don't use it for anything else, unless the law requires it; then we tell you first, unless the law forbids that. If we believe an instruction breaks the GDPR, we tell you.

3. Confidentiality

Only people who need it to run the Service have access to the data, and they are bound to keep it confidential.

4. Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures for the risks involved. At least:

  • All connections use TLS (HTTPS).
  • Passwords are stored as argon2id hashes; sign-in sessions and agent tokens are stored as hashes too.
  • Every workspace has its own database file; one workspace can't reach another's data.
  • Servers run in the EU (Hetzner, Germany). The hosting dashboard isn't reachable from the internet, and only the operator has access to the servers.
  • Services run as unprivileged containers. Every change passes automated tests, including access-control tests, before it is deployed.
  • Sign-in attempts are rate-limited; content from users is always shown as text, never as HTML.
  • Software and dependencies are kept up to date; security reports are handled with priority.

We may change these measures, as long as the level of security doesn't go down.

5. Subprocessors

We give each subprocessor the same data protection obligations as this DPA. We tell you by email at least 30 days before we add or replace one. If you object for good reasons and we can't resolve it, you can end the agreement before the change. We remain responsible for our subprocessors.

6. Transfers outside the EU

We transfer personal data outside the European Economic Area only with the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (for Resend: their DPA).

7. Helping you

  • Your admins can handle most requests from people themselves: correct names, remove members, and delete comments and threads. For anything else, such as fully erasing a person or exporting their data, ask us and we help.
  • If a person contacts us directly about your workspace, we forward the request to you and don't answer it ourselves unless you ask.
  • On request, we help with data protection impact assessments and consultations with a supervisory authority, as far as they concern our processing.
  • For help that takes more than a reasonable amount of work, we may charge reasonable costs, agreed with you in advance.

8. Data breaches

If we become aware of a data breach affecting your data, we tell you without undue delay and, where possible, within 48 hours, by email to the workspace's billing contact. We include what we know about its nature, the data and people affected, the likely consequences and the measures taken, and add to it as we learn more. We take measures to limit the damage and help you meet your own obligations to report it.

9. When the agreement ends

During the 30 days after the agreement ends, you can ask for an export of your workspace. After that, we delete the workspace and the personal data in it within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it.

10. Showing that we comply

We give you the information you need to check that we keep to this DPA, such as answers to a reasonable security questionnaire. If that isn't enough, an independent auditor bound to confidentiality may audit us, at most once a year, with 30 days' notice, during working hours and at your cost, unless the audit shows that we seriously failed to comply.

11. Liability

The limits on liability in section 10 of the terms also apply to this DPA, as far as the law allows.
