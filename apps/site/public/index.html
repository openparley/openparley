Delivery

Set up in two steps

Add the script tag from your workspace to your site. Only signed-in teammates load OpenParley; every other visitor downloads a 1 KB loader.

<script src="https://you.openparley.dev/openparley.js"
        data-project="…" data-launcher="members" defer></script>

Then connect your coding agent. It signs in through your browser the first time.

claude mcp add --transport http openparley https://you.openparley.dev/mcp

Or hand both steps to your coding agent: every project in your workspace has a setup prompt to paste into Claude Code, Cursor or Codex. It puts the tag where your framework defines the page head and connects itself (how it works).
