Privacy policy

Last updated {{legal.updated}}

We collect as little as we can. openparley.dev has no analytics, no tracking and no cookies, and loads nothing from other companies. This page explains what we do process, why, and what you can ask of us.

Who we are

OpenParley is run by {{legal.providerWithAddress}}. For anything about your data, email {{legal.email}}.

We have two roles:

  • Controller for this website, for signing up for and paying for OpenParley Cloud, and for email you send us. This policy covers those.
  • Processor for what's inside an OpenParley Cloud workspace: accounts, threads, comments and notification emails. The organization that runs the workspace decides about that data, under our data processing agreement. If you were invited to a workspace, ask that organization first; we help them answer you. What we store for them is listed below as well.

If you run OpenParley on your own server, or use it without a server, we receive nothing at all: the data stays on your server or in the browser.

Visiting openparley.dev

  • No cookies, analytics, ads or third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Like any website, our servers receive your IP address and the page you ask for, to send it to you. We don't keep logs of visits. Our hosting infrastructure may keep short-lived technical logs, including IP addresses, for security and to fix problems; they are deleted within 14 days. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website.
  • The demo on the homepage runs only in your browser. Threads you make there are saved in your browser's local storage and are never sent to us. A thread's Copy link puts the conversation in the part of the link after #, which browsers don't send to servers.

Signing up for OpenParley Cloud

  • What: the workspace name and address you choose, your email address, and the plan. While you sign up, a cookie (openparley_signup, 7 days) makes sure only your browser is shown your workspace's setup link.
  • Why: to create your workspace, send you the confirmation and setup emails, and contact you about your workspace and plan (the email address becomes the workspace's billing contact). Legal basis: performing our agreement with you.
  • Polar: when you confirm a workspace or start a paid plan, we give Polar Software, Inc. your email address, workspace name and workspace address, so you can upgrade and manage billing. Polar is our merchant of record: it sells you the subscription and processes payment details, tax details and invoices as a separate controller, under Polar's privacy policy. We never see your card details. Polar sends us updates about your subscription, which we keep as billing records.
  • How long: sign-ups that never become a workspace are deleted 30 days after the link expires. The billing contact is kept while the workspace exists. Billing records are kept for seven years, because Dutch tax law requires it.

Email with us

If you write to us, we use your message and email address to answer you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in helping you, or our agreement with you. We delete support email two years after the conversation ends, unless we need it longer for a legal reason.

Inside a workspace (as processor)

For the organization that runs a workspace, we store and process:

  • Accounts: name, email address, mention name, role, and the password as an argon2id hash (never the password itself). Sign-in sessions and agent tokens are stored only as hashes.
  • Threads: the comments, who wrote them (a person or their coding agent), when, and the page and element a thread is on: the page path, the element's selector, up to 120 characters of its text and a few of its attributes (such as its id, label or link), and the environment and revision your site reports.
  • Notifications: notification settings, and emails waiting to be sent, which are deleted within 24 hours.
  • IP addresses are used to limit sign-in attempts. They are held in memory only and are never written to disk.

When an admin removes a member, that member can no longer sign in and their sessions and agent tokens are deleted. Their name stays on comments they wrote, so the conversation still makes sense. To be erased completely, ask the workspace's admin, or us.

Who else receives data

We don't sell data or use it for advertising. We use these providers:

  • Hetzner Online GmbH, Germany: our servers, in Nuremberg, Germany. All data is stored there.
  • Resend, Inc., United States: sends our emails, including notification emails, which contain the comment that triggered them.
  • Polar Software, Inc., United States: merchant of record for paid plans (see above).

Where data goes to the United States, this happens under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

Security

All connections are encrypted, passwords and tokens are stored as hashes, and every workspace has its own database. The DPA lists our security measures.

Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, and to correct, delete, restrict or transfer it, and you can object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Email {{legal.email}}; we answer within a month. We make no automated decisions about you. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or the authority where you live.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update the date at the top. We email workspace billing contacts about important changes before they take effect.
